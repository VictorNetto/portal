# Introduction

This portal is where the Red Team keeps what it finds and delivers what it found. Each security flaw discovered in an operation becomes a **vulnerability** in the portal. The work of a whole operation becomes a **report**, which brings together the story of the work, the vulnerabilities found and the evidence. When a report or a vulnerability is ready, the portal delivers it to the team responsible and then follows the fix through to the end.

This guide explains how to do each of those things. It is written to be read in order the first time, but each page also makes sense on its own, for when you come back with a specific question. The chapters follow a finding's path through the portal: first signing in and finding your way around, then vulnerabilities, the reports that group them and the templates that speed up the writing, and then the tools to find, export and audit what already exists. The last chapters cover more specific subjects, such as the API for other systems, dashboards and running the portal, and only appear for those who can use them.

## How to read this guide

The bar on the left shows every chapter and its sections. You can jump straight to any page from there, or read in order with the **Previous** and **Next** buttons at the bottom of each page; the ← and → keys do the same. The search box at the top of the bar searches the whole guide.

The guide only shows what your role lets you use. If a colleague mentions something you can't find here, it most likely belongs to another role; [Roles and access](#papeis) explains which ones exist.

Throughout the text, button and field names appear in **bold**, as they do on screen, and what you type appears in `fixed type`, like `status:sent`.
